# Changed-in period: items touched in the period keep their colour, the rest go grey

The side menu has a new **changed:** picker next to period (two days, the same control as the item page dates). While a period is set, every card on the board, every row stripe in the list and every bar and dot on the calendar that belongs to an item created or changed in it (an event in its worklog, a note, its creation day, or a file write) keeps its colour; everything else is grey. The legend starts with "changed 1 Nov – 7 Nov" and lists only the colours still in use. The × beside the picker clears it; the choice is remembered per board.

Checked in the real app on the hidden desktop, light and dark: with 1–7 Nov set, 15 of 34 cards kept their colour and the list showed the same 15 rows coloured; the calendar coloured 6 of 17 bars; clearing restored all.

Still open: the three git-based subtasks from the item's earlier version (uncommitted changes, one commit, between two commits). They are a different mechanism (git diff rather than the worklog); say whether they still matter.
