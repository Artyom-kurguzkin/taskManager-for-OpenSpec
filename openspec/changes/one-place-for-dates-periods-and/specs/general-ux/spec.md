## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Dates, periods and colours read the same on every screen
Every screen SHALL write a date, a period and a field colour the same way, in the user's culture, and SHALL count today from the app's clock.

#### Scenario: Period across a year end
- **WHEN** a period runs from 28 Dec to 3 Jan
- **THEN** the period picker, the side menu and the card all show it with both years
