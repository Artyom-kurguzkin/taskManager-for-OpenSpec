# Decision

The server's lifetime is the app's: the app starts it with `--parent <pid>` and it exits when that process ends; the shim only connects and never spawns one.
Why: one owner, nothing left holding dist after the app closes, and a crash cleans up too. Ceiling: with two app processes open, closing the first stops the server until the second restarts it (it re-ensures when Settings or a reconnect asks).
