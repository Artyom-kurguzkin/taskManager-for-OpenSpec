# Columns, rows and colour, the same in every view

The side menu has an "Organise" block with three independent choices: columns, rows and colour. Each can be any of status, feature, label, priority, contributor, due date or start date (rows and colour can also be none). The board shows the columns across and a row per value of the row field; dropping a card into a cell sets both its column field and its row field. Cards, list rows and roadmap bars take their colour from the colour field, and a legend under it names the colours. The list and the roadmap group by rows, then columns, the same way.

A due-date or start-date period cannot be set by a drop: the card stays put for that dimension and a message says so. Feature and contributor drops replace the value the card came from, as feature and contributor columns already did. Checked in the real app, light and dark: columns by status, rows by feature, colour by label; a card dragged from the Unassigned row of Backlog into the Maintainability row of Planned got status Planned and feature Maintainability on disk.
