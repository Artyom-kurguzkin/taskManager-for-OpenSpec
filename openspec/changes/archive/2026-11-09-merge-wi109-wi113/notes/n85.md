# Swimlanes on the board, the list and the roadmap

The side menu has a "swimlanes" option: none, priority or label. On the board each value is a row across every column, with the column headers once on top; dropping a card into a row's column sets its status and that field. The list groups its rows by the swimlane value above the column, and the roadmap groups its items by it. "+ Add subgroup", "Remove subgroup" and the automatic High / Lower priority subgroups are gone from the board. Checked in the real app, light and dark: with swimlanes by priority the board showed High priority (5) and Lower priority (28) rows, the roadmap grouped by the same two, and a card dragged from Lower / Backlog into High / In Progress changed both its status and its priority on disk.

Rough edges to know about: a swimlane row is as tall as its tallest cell, so a row whose cards sit in a far column leaves empty space; in the list an empty swimlane and column pair shows as a "No items" group; the WorkItems module still stores the old per-column subgroups (x-rtm.lanes in config.yaml and on items) though nothing on the board uses them any more.
