# 31 Oct — one selection look

Cards, list rows, project headings and subtasks now share one selection look: the item keeps its surface, a solid line in the text colour hugs it, a dotted yellow line runs round that (rows draw both inside their edge). The yellow fills and tints are gone. Checked in the real app, light and dark.
