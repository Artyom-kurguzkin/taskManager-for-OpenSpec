# Mechanism, fix and green

Mechanism: BoardOptions.ShowHidden ("show all hidden") is saved per repository and, when on, keeps hidden columns and projects in view by design (Concealed is false, the page also reads the hidden projects). The hide itself is saved (the Hidden list shows it) but nothing on the screen changed, so every hide looked dead until the switch was off. Why a restart seemed to fix it I could not reproduce: the switch survives a restart, so it was probably switched off on the way. I tried the page's own Set (header menu), the side menu's Set, the debounced SaveBoardSoon, the grid-settings save, arrow moves, an item round trip, board/list switches: the page's options and the settings never diverged, so there is no race or leak in the re-projection path.

Fix (the only way a hide can now look like nothing): a column or project hidden and kept in view by the switch is marked, its header dashed and dimmed with "(hidden)" after the name (class is-hidden, app.css). Tests: HidingInTheAppTests, hide after an arrow move and a status change for a column and a project, and the marking under show all hidden.
