# Reproduction over CDP: the hide works; with show all hidden on it only looks broken

Over CDP (hidden app, two projects, port 9533) a column hide through the header menu took effect right after start, after arrow-key card moves, after opening an item and coming back, and a restore through the side menu's eye worked every time; no sequence made it stop. What does reproduce the owner's screenshot exactly is the persisted "show all hidden" switch being on: the column is saved as hidden (the Hidden list shows "Completed 37" with its eye) but, by that switch's own rule, it stays drawn, and nothing on it says it is hidden. In that state every hide (column, project) looks like it does nothing.

```
4 show all off cols=["Backlog","Planned","Confirmed","In Progress","In Review","Research"] hiddenList=["Blocked","Completed"]
(3, with show all on) cols still hold Blocked and Completed, hiddenList=["Blocked","Completed"], saved=status:Blocked,status:Completed
```

I found no race: the page's options and the settings agree after every path I drove (the page's own Set, the side menu's Set, the debounced save, the grid settings save). Fix: a hidden column or project that is kept in view by the switch is marked as hidden, so the hide is visible whatever the switch says.
