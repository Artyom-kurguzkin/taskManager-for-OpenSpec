## Why

The calendar was a month grid with stacked bars. GitHub Projects' roadmap, with the items listed on the left and a timeline on the right, grouped the way the board is grouped, reads better and scales to more than a month.

## What Changes

- The Calendar view becomes a roadmap: left pane the items in the board's groups (group by), right pane a timeline with a day (Month zoom) or week (Quarter zoom) scale, bars from start to due coloured by status, a bar per group spanning the group's dates, a today line, and Today / previous / next / zoom controls. Rows and bars open the item.
- The month grid and its "No dates" aside are gone; undated items are listed with no bar.
