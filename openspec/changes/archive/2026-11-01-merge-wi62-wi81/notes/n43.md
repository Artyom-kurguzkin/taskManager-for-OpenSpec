# 1 Nov — archive never stops on a stale delta

Completing an item used to fail when another archive had added scenarios to a requirement the item also modifies. Now the app refreshes the item's delta specs first: the scenarios the main spec gained are appended to the modified block, verbatim, and the archive goes through. Proven against the real OpenSpec CLI.
