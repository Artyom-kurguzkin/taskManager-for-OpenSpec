# Merged from #wi91 agent in work item title

## Why

The "agent" chip in a work item's title row wasted space and said nothing useful. Who did the work belongs with the contributors.

## What Changes

- The title row has no agent chip. An agent that stamped the item appears among the contributors as a lilac chip; while the work is unreviewed that chip is the review control (click → reviewed), afterwards a plain lilac chip.
- `openspec/REPO-TASKS.md` tells agents to add their name to `contributors` when they create a work item or subtask.
- Cards keep their small agent tag; subtask and note chips are unchanged.
