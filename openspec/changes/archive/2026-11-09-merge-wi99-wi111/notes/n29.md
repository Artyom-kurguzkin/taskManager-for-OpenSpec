# 31 Oct — features, not buckets

"Bucket" is called feature everywhere, and grouped by feature the board is one flat row: no supergroup headings, Unassigned first, "+ feature" at the end. Every feature can be deleted from its column menu; a spec-backed one goes with its folder, unless an open change still has a spec for it. A board saved as "bucket" opens as "feature".
