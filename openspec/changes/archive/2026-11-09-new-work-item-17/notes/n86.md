# An open item sits beside the list

On a window at least 1400px wide, opening a work item shows the list on the left and the item on the right. The open item's row is tinted, clicking another row opens it on the right while the list keeps its scroll and filters, and a toggle at the top of the item page hides the list; the choice is remembered in settings. Narrower windows show only the item. Checked in the real app at 1920px, light and dark: the split was up a second after opening, the row was marked, a click on another row opened it in 3 ms with the same list element and scroll position, and the toggle hid the list and saved Split=false.
