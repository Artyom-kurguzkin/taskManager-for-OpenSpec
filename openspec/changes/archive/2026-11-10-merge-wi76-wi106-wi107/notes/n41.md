# 1 Nov — calendar, dates, periods

Work items now have a start date (stamped the first time an item moves to In Progress) and a due date, both set on the item page. The Layout menu has a Calendar: a month in weeks, each dated item a bar from start to due, coloured by status, and open items without dates listed at the side. The board and list group by due date or start date, and the side menu has a period filter (overdue, today, this week, this month, no due date). Cards show the due date, red when overdue; the list has Started and Due columns. Checked on the hidden desktop, light and dark.
