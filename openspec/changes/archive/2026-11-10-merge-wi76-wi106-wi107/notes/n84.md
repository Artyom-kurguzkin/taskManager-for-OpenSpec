# The calendar is a roadmap now

The Calendar view copies the GitHub Projects roadmap from the screenshot: the items on the left in the board's groups, each group with a chevron, name and count; a timeline on the right with month names and day ticks, a line on today, a bar per dated item from start to due coloured by status (dashed where the window cuts it), and a bar per group spanning the group's dates with the range as text. Month zoom shows three months of days, Quarter zoom two quarters of weeks; Today, previous and next move the window. Rows and bars open the item. The month grid is gone. Checked in the real app, light and dark: a 7-day item was 7 day-columns wide, the today line sat on 7 Nov, and the left and right rows lined up.
