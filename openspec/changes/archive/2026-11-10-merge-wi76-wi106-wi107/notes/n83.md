# Transition date on every item

A work item now carries x-rtm.moved, the day it last moved to its status column. Creating an item stamps it; every status change restamps it; setting the same status again leaves it. A change without the key reads as moved on its creation date, and nothing is written until the status changes. The item page shows "in <status> since <date>" beside the dates, and the list has an "In status since" column after Due. Checked in the real app: changing a demo item to Blocked turned the chip into "in Blocked since 7 Nov" and wrote moved: 2026-11-07 to its file.
