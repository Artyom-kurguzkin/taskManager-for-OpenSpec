# Merged from #wi123 List shows where its rows are, and rows drag from anywhere

## Why

Scrolled down the list, rows give no hint of which column and row group they are in; the title column is indented; rows drag only by a six-dot grip.

## What Changes

- The sticky header names the column and row group (and the project, when several are open) of the rows scrolled under it.
- No grip column; titles line up under the Title header.
- A row drags from anywhere after a short press-and-hold; a click still opens or acts, and a quick press-and-move still selects cells.
