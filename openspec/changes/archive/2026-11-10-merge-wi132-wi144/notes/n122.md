# Every list column can be hidden again

The × was on every column, but in a column narrower than its label it sat past the edge of the header's title box, which clips what overflows; clicks there went to the invisible reorder layer under it. The label now shortens with an ellipsis instead, so the × stays inside. Checked in the real app at 1920 wide: a click on the × of every one of the eleven headers reaches the button.
