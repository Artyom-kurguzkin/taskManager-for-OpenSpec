# List cells stay in their column

Every list cell now keeps its content inside its own column. Text that doesn't fit ends in an ellipsis and shows in full when you hover. Label, priority and feature pills shrink, and the progress bar shrinks before its count is cut. The Label column starts wide enough for "enhancement". Measured in the real app: 107 cells spilled over before, none after, at default and at very narrow widths, in light and dark.
