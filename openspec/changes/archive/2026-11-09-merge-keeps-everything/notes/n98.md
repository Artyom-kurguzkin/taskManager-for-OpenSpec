# Merged content is grouped by source and date

A merge now heads each source's task group with its number, title and merge date ("#wi64 Notes editor · merged 2026-11-09") and stamps the notes it moves in with the source number and merge date. On the item page the Subtasks tile shows every group as a collapsible header with its progress; a fully done group starts collapsed. The Change log shows the item's own notes first, then a collapsed section per merged source, newest first, headed "From #wiN · merged 9 Nov". Checked in the real app, light and dark, by merging three demo items.

Items merged before this change have no merge stamps, so their notes still show as the merged item's own and their groups keep their old headings.
