# The list says where you are, and rows drag from anywhere

- The sticky header has a line above the column titles naming the group of the rows under it, e.g. "Planned › Track work items" (with the project first when several are open). It is empty at the top of the list.
- The grip column is gone and titles start right under the Title header; the subtask chevron sits in the gap to the left of the title.
- A row drags from anywhere: press, hold a quarter second (the row lifts and the cursor becomes a grabbing hand), then move. A plain click opens or acts as before; pressing and moving at once still selects cells. A dragged row moves up and down only.

Checked in the real app on the hidden desktop, light and dark: the path while scrolled and with two projects, titles aligned, a hold-drag onto Planned that changed the item's status on disk, a quick drag that selected cells, a click that opened the item. Touch was not tried.
