# 31 Oct — hints behind a setting

Keyboard hints (F) are off until switched on in Settings → Keyboard hints. The layout pushes the switch to the page script with the theme; off, F is a plain letter and any open badges close. Checked in the real app: nothing on F by default, 19 badges after the switch, nothing again after switching off.
