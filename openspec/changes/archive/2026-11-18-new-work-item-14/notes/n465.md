# Decision

The Work items / Subtasks unit switch leaves the Filters section; the examined item's panel lists its subtasks directly. The board's own subtask view (every item's subtasks as cards) is a different thing and stays: a "All subtasks on the board" link in the panel turns it on, and Filters shows the switch only while it is on, as the way back.
Why: removing it outright would strand a user in the subtask board with no way out.
