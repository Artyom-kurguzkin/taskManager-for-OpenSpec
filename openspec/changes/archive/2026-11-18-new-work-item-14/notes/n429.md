# Decision

The examined item is one nullable (root, id) in UiService; a plain click on a card or list row examines it only while the menu is expanded and the list is not the split view's (that list keeps opening at once).
It is a UI-session state, not a setting, so it clears on navigation, on collapsing the menu and on a click that is not on a work item (board background, calendar background, any click in the menu outside the panel).
