# Decision

Provider and address are saved in openspec/config.yaml under x-rtm.boardStorage, not in REPO-TASKS.md.
REPO-TASKS.md is generated by the app and rewritten when it ships a newer version, so a setting in it would be overwritten; config.yaml's x-rtm block already holds the repository's app settings and has a format-preserving writer.
