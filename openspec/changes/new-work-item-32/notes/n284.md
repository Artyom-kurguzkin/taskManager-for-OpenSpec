# Decision

App edits are the paths OpenSpecStoreApi.ChangesSince reports (every own write is recorded there); the board server, another process, appends the paths it wrote to server-writes.log in the data folder, which the desktop service reads and clears at each sync.
Git's and editors' writes are in neither, so they stay outside edits; no hook in OpenSpecStore was needed.
