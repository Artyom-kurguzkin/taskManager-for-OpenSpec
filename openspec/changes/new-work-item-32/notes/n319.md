# Sync triggers cut to three: start, exit, two quiet minutes after a change

The app syncs when it starts, when it closes, and two minutes after the last change (each change restarts the wait); "Sync now" stays. The 2-second debounce, the 45-second timer, the back-off and the idle tracking are removed, with the tests that only checked their timing.
The owner wants the cheapest working solution, not live collaboration: an early or late sync is put right by the next one. A computer left open and idle receives nothing until its next change, restart or Sync now.
