# A broken yaml no longer kills the app

An unquoted colon in a hand-written title made the app die at startup, before its window. Now a change whose yaml does not parse shows on the board with a red ! and a warning naming the file and the line ("line 10: mapping values are not allowed in this context; fix it and the item's fields will show"); its metadata cannot be written until it is fixed; a note with a broken frontmatter shows its body with a warning in its title; files with mixed line endings read; a startup error goes to a crash log under RepoTaskManager/logs instead of a message box that cannot show yet. Checked in the real app on the hidden desktop, light and dark, with one broken change: the app started, the board showed every item, the broken one with its warning.
