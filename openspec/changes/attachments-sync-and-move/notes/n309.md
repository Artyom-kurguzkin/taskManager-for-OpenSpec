# WholeFilesOnly skips the marker scan but keeps the git-operation check

With WholeFilesOnly the conflict-marker scan never runs, even with a GitFolder; a merge or rebase in progress under that GitFolder still pauses the sync.
The brief only asked to disable the marker scan, and the host is expected to pass no GitFolder for attachments anyway.
