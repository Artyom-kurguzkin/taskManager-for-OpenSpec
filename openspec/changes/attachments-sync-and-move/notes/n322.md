Attachments sync to `attachments/` in the connected storage in the same pass as the board (start, exit, two quiet minutes after a change), stay out of git, and can be switched off per project. The attachments folder is a project setting; changing it asks, moves the files and rewrites paths; a change from another computer is followed. Whole fast suite in the main tree, one run:
