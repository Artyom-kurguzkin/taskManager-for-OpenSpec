# Swimlane rows can be selected, moved and hidden

Shift-click (or Ctrl/⌘-click) a swimlane header to select it; several can be selected. The up and down arrow keys move the selected rows past their neighbours, and dragging a header moves it. Hover a header for its ×, which hides the row; the side menu's Hidden list shows it with its count and the eye brings it back. The order and the hidden rows are remembered per board and row field, and the list and calendar follow the same order. Checked in the real app, light and dark, with rows by label: moved, restarted, dragged, hidden and restored.

Columns already drag and hide (through "Hide column" in their header menu), but they do not yet take Shift-click selection or arrow-key moves: a header click opens its menu, and left/right already move cards between columns. That is left open as task 1.3.
