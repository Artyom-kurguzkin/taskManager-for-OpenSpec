# Cause found over CDP (hidden app, list: groups status, subgroups priority)

Reproduced with real CDP key events: Shift-click the first row of a subgroup, ArrowUp: the key does reach BoardPage.KeyDown (app.js forwards it), but nothing visible moves. Cause: KeyDown (BoardPage.razor.cs:592-602) and EdgeMove steps in the whole status column (`p.Flat` columns), not in the subgroup (the swim row's cell); the row above in the column belongs to another subgroup, so the step swaps with an invisible neighbour and EdgeMove never sees the subgroup edge. The board with rows has the same cause (cards step past cards of another row), and its selected card also scrolls out of view as it moves (the board's scrollTop is never adjusted); the FLIP animation itself is fine once prefers-reduced-motion is off (the hidden desktop reports reduce, which app.css honours).
