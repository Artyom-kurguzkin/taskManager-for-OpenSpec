# Root cause found over CDP (hidden app, list grouped by status): a mouse press on

Root cause found over CDP (hidden app, list grouped by status): a mouse press on a row's cell padding and a 48 px move gives `is-dragging: false`, `td.cs` count 2 (cell selection), no Radzen handler involved. In app.js a row press only becomes a drag after HOLD 250 ms without moving; moving earlier calls `_endPress()` and the cell-selection handlers (`_cells`) take the press, whatever is under the pointer. Fix: decide by what is under the pointer at press time (text vs. not text) in the shared pointerdown handlers.
