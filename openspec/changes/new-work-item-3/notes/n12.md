# 26 Oct — columns in the list view

What the list offers now: hide a column from the × in its header (it joins the side menu's Hidden list, with an eye to bring it back — the same mechanism as board columns, which also show how many items they hold); reorder a column by dragging its header; resize it from the handle on its right edge. Column widths, order, sort and filters are remembered per repository. Groups collapse from their own chevron.
