# Hand-off: click selects, click on the selected item or double-click opens, Shift extends

Staged in the index, nothing committed (1b64f33 from agent/click-select). UI-only: no unit tests by the owner's rule; checked in the real app over CDP, board, list and calendar, light and dark (shots under the removed worktree's tests/out/ver235, listed in the feed).

**Rules as built**
- A plain click on a card, list row or calendar bar makes that item the only selected one (project, row and column selections are let go too); with the side menu expanded it also examines the item. It no longer opens it.
- A plain click on the item that is the whole selection opens its details; a double-click on an unselected item opens it (first click selects, second opens) — one open either way.
- Shift / Ctrl / ⌘-click adds or removes the item, as before; new in the calendar, which had no selection at all (bars and left rows now carry the `selected` ring).
- A click on empty space lets go of the item selection (new in the board and list background handlers and in the calendar).
- Headings, swimlane and column headers, arrow keys and drag are unchanged.

**Notes**: opening an item recreates the board page, so coming back leaves nothing selected; examine-on-click with the menu expanded was not exercised in the app.
